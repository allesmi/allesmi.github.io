title: "Why sunbeng?"
---

This website has the unusual name 'sunbeng'. To understand the _why_ we first look at the _what_.

A sunbeng is a colloquial (Upper-) Austrian word for a bench in the sun.

The fond memory of the countless times I have relaxed on a sunbeng and the fact that it does not sound too bad in English either lead me to the decision to choose this word as my domain name.
